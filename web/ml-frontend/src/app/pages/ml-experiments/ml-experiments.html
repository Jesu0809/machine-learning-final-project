Lesson: a suspiciously good result

v1 caught the expert survivor more often than the novice. Before trusting it we checked for an information leak (the observations never include the player's position, and the model gives the same rates outside Unity). The real cause was the opponent: the bot froze whenever Freddy was close, and the expert, who read the tracker more, froze more. Freddy learned to exploit the bot, not to hunt. We fixed the bot and retrained. In reinforcement learning, the agent learns whatever the environment rewards.

Next: does he learn where you hide?

planned

The survivor bot has a hiding habit (how often it picks the locker over other spots), randomized every training night. The plan is to evaluate the trained brain against survivors with a fixed strong habit and measure whether it opens the habitual spot first more often, and to compare against an ablation trained without the habit randomization.
