Freddy has learned to hunt.

A first-person horror game in an abandoned pizzeria. The animatronic that hunts you is not scripted: his brain is a neural network trained with reinforcement learning (PPO) in millions of simulated decisions. He only knows what he hears and sees, and he learned on his own where to look, which lockers to open and when to use his sense.
