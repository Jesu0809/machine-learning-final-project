Tips

  • The objectives appear when the call ends: use the call to learn the building.
  • Doors can be closed behind you (the dim lamp over them turns red); he has to break them down.
  • His brain cannot see through walls. It hears running, fuses, the breaker and the exit panel.
  • Crouched in the dark with the flashlight off, he only sees you up close.
  • If you panic he can sense you. His network decides when.
  • Press Tab (View on a controller): the network's probabilities are right there.
