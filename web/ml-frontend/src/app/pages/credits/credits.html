Pockets

Pockets (his head, body, mallet and textures) was sculpted for this project by a script (tools/sculpt_pockets.py): no third-party model is part of him. The dummies and spare heads around the circus are a light version of the same sculpt. Rigged and animated with Adobe Mixamo. His cloth and metal use scanned surfaces from Poly Haven (CC0).

Props, surfaces and posters

  • Props and scanned surfaces from Poly Haven and ambientCG (CC0). The striped circus canvas is printed and aged over Poly Haven's "Rough Linen".
  • The wall posters use details of public-domain circus lithographs (about 1890–1900) from Wikimedia Commons, cropped so no show's name remains and set under new type.
  • Fonts from Google Fonts: Oswald, Rye, Bebas Neue, Bungee and VT323 (SIL Open Font License); Luckiest Guy, Special Elite, Permanent Marker and Schoolbell (Apache License 2.0).

Sounds and voices

  • The clown's toys, surprise boxes, shoes, bells and music box: CC0 recordings from Freesound by Breviceps, Aiyumi, rschiwal, D.jones, shakaharu, romulofs, JarredGibb and Modification1089.
  • His steps, servos and creaks: CC0 recordings by Thimras (OpenGameArt), JoontheFloof, ingudios and LoafDV (Freesound).

  • Voices synthesized locally with Kokoro (Apache License 2.0). Every other sound was synthesized for this project.
