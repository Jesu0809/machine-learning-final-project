Training

Freddy trains against a simulated survivor that collects fuses, hides and runs. At the start he wanders at random and loses most nights; after a few million decisions he catches the survivor almost every night.

The training opponent

A rule-based survivor bot plays every training night. It goes for the fuses and the exit, avoiding the room where its tracker last showed Freddy. Every night its personality is random (domain randomization):

  • How often it reads the motion tracker (50–97%).
  • Whether it hides or runs when he is close (hides 40–80%).
  • How often it runs and makes noise (5–45%).
  • How long it waits before risking a path next to him, and which hiding spot it prefers.

Evaluation uses three fixed presets: Novice, Normal and Expert.

Reproduce: mlagents-learn config/freddy_ppo.yaml --run-id=freddy_v9, then mlagents-learn config/freddy_ppo_finetune.yaml --run-id=freddy_v10 --initialize-from=freddy_v9, and press Play on the HuntTraining scene.
