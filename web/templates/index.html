The player

Never moves. Checks five cameras, opens and closes two doors, and has limited power. Closed doors and open cameras drain it faster.

The monster

Jumps between connected rooms every few seconds. If it reaches a door that is open, it gets into the office and the night is over.

The learning

Trained offline against thousands of simulated guards. It sees how long ago each camera was checked, so it reacts to your habits as you play.
