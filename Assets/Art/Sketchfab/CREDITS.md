# Sketchfab models

Prepared for the game by `tools/prepare_models.py` in the FNAF-ML repository (rescaled to meters,
facing +Z, moving parts split into their own nodes, textures resized). Imported with glTFast.

- `car_wagon.glb`: "Kiri '86 - Low poly model" by Daniel Zhabotinsky, CC BY 4.0, without its badges,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/kiri-86-low-poly-model-5c17c582ef9549798f694b660feea42a
- `car_wreck.glb`: "Destroyed Car 03 / Backrooms Car (Gameready ver)" by Renafox (@kryik1023), CC BY 4.0,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/destroyed-car-03-backrooms-car-gameready-ver-efc4dfe2c7284a64bc9281eb4f61d402
- `exit_lever.glb`: "Industrial Electricity's Lever" by Amine.Elouneg, CC BY 4.0,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/66915915e3234d319415d6bfb4e3a59e
- `knife_switch.glb`: "Early 1900s Knife Switches Type C" by Mad_Lobster_Workshop, CC BY 4.0,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/033dd717e4b04e46b6da85195085b8f3
